Full-day educational courses on whole-brain, connectome-based models of brain dynamics, run at the
annual meeting of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM). Each course combines tutorial
lectures with hands-on sessions working through pre-prepared tutorial code.

### 2025: Neural Field Theory for Whole-Brain Modelling

One-day educational course at OHBM 2025. JG was co-lead instructor and course developer.
[Course site](https://davi1990.github.io/OHBM2025-NFT-Course/) ·
[GitHub repo](https://github.com/GriffithsLab/OHBM2025-NFT-Course)

### 2024: Whole-brain, Connectome-based Models of Brain Dynamics: From Principles to Applications

One-day educational course at OHBM 2024, Seoul, South Korea. JG was lead instructor and course developer.
[Course site](https://griffithslab.github.io/OHBM2024-educational-course/)

### 2023: Whole-brain, Connectome-based Models of Brain Dynamics: From Principles to Applications

One-day educational course at OHBM 2023, Montreal, Canada, organized by Davide Momi, John Griffiths and
Joana Cabral. JG was lead instructor and course developer. Sections covered background and theory,
systems/cognitive applications, and clinical applications, each followed by a hands-on session.
[Course site](https://griffithslab.github.io/OHBM-whole-brain-modelling-course/) ·
[GitHub repo](https://github.com/GriffithsLab/OHBM-whole-brain-modelling-course)
